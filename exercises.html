Push-Ups Beginner

Hands shoulder-width apart, body in a straight line. Lower your chest to the floor, then push back up. Keep your core tight throughout.

Incline Push-Ups Beginner

Hands on a raised surface (chair, step). Lowers difficulty while still targeting the chest — good starting point.

Diamond Push-Ups Intermediate

Hands together under your chest forming a diamond shape. Emphasizes inner chest and triceps.

Decline Push-Ups Advanced

Feet raised on a surface, hands on the floor. Shifts more weight onto the upper chest and shoulders.

Superman Hold Beginner

Lie face down, lift arms and legs off the ground simultaneously, hold briefly, then lower. Strengthens the lower back.

Reverse Snow Angels Beginner

Lie face down, sweep arms from your sides up overhead and back, like a snow angel. Targets upper back and rear delts.

Towel Rows Intermediate

Loop a towel around a sturdy pole/door, lean back, and pull yourself toward it using your back muscles.

Pull-Ups (doorframe bar) Advanced

Using a doorframe pull-up bar, pull your chin above the bar with control, then lower slowly.

Bodyweight Squats Beginner

Feet shoulder-width apart, lower your hips back and down like sitting in a chair, then stand back up.

Lunges Beginner

Step forward, lower your back knee toward the floor, then push back to standing. Alternate legs.

Bulgarian Split Squats Intermediate

Rear foot elevated on a chair, lower your front knee until it's bent around 90 degrees, then push up.

Jump Squats Advanced

Perform a squat, then explode upward into a jump, landing softly back into the squat position.

Chair Dips Beginner

Hands on the edge of a sturdy chair, legs extended, lower your body by bending your elbows, then push back up. Targets triceps.

Wall/Table Curls Beginner

Push against the underside of a sturdy table with your palms up as if curling, holding tension in the biceps.

Pike Push-Ups Intermediate

Hips raised high forming an inverted V, lower your head toward the floor, then push back up. Targets shoulders and triceps.

Diamond Push-Up Hold Advanced

Hold the bottom position of a diamond push-up for time to build tricep endurance and strength.

Plank Beginner

Forearms and toes on the ground, body in a straight line. Hold, keeping your hips level and core braced.

Dead Bug Beginner

Lying on your back, extend opposite arm and leg slowly while keeping your lower back pressed to the floor.

Bicycle Crunches Intermediate

Lying on your back, alternate bringing elbow to opposite knee in a pedaling motion.

Hollow Body Hold Advanced

Lying on your back, lift shoulders and legs off the ground into a curved "hollow" shape, and hold.

Burpees Intermediate

Drop into a squat, kick back into a plank, do a push-up, jump feet back in, then jump up. Full-body conditioning.

Mountain Climbers Beginner

In a plank position, drive knees toward your chest alternately at a quick pace.

Jumping Jacks Beginner

Jump feet out while raising arms overhead, then jump back to start. Great warm-up or cardio finisher.

Bear Crawl Advanced

On hands and feet, knees hovering just above the ground, crawl forward and backward with control.
