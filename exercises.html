Push-Ups Beginner

Hands shoulder-width apart, body in a straight line. Lower your chest to the floor, then push back up. Keep your core tight throughout.

Incline Push-Ups Beginner

Hands on a raised surface (chair, step). Lowers difficulty while still targeting the chest — good starting point.

Wide Push-Ups Beginner

Hands set wider than shoulder-width. Emphasizes the outer chest more than a standard push-up.

Diamond Push-Ups Intermediate

Hands together under your chest forming a diamond shape. Emphasizes inner chest and triceps.

Staggered Push-Ups Intermediate

One hand slightly forward of the other. Shifts load unevenly to challenge each side differently.

Decline Push-Ups Advanced

Feet raised on a surface, hands on the floor. Shifts more weight onto the upper chest and shoulders.

Archer Push-Ups Advanced

Shift your weight to one arm while the other stays extended out to the side. Builds unilateral chest strength.

Push-Up to Plank Reach Advanced

After a push-up, reach one hand forward from a plank position, alternating sides. Adds a stability challenge.

Superman Hold Beginner

Lie face down, lift arms and legs off the ground simultaneously, hold briefly, then lower. Strengthens the lower back.

Reverse Snow Angels Beginner

Lie face down, sweep arms from your sides up overhead and back, like a snow angel. Targets upper back and rear delts.

Prone Y-Raises Beginner

Lie face down, arms extended in a Y shape, lift them slightly off the ground and hold.

Towel Rows Intermediate

Loop a towel around a sturdy pole/door, lean back, and pull yourself toward it using your back muscles.

Doorframe Rows Intermediate

Hold a doorframe edge, lean back with straight arms, then pull your chest toward the frame.

Scapular Pulls Intermediate

From a hang or lean position, pull your shoulder blades together without bending the elbows.

Pull-Ups (doorframe bar) Advanced

Using a doorframe pull-up bar, pull your chin above the bar with control, then lower slowly.

Renegade Rows (using bottles) Advanced

From a plank holding two weighted bottles, row one up to your ribcage at a time while keeping your hips stable.

Bodyweight Squats Beginner

Feet shoulder-width apart, lower your hips back and down like sitting in a chair, then stand back up.

Lunges Beginner

Step forward, lower your back knee toward the floor, then push back to standing. Alternate legs.

Glute Bridges Beginner

Lying on your back, knees bent, lift your hips toward the ceiling by squeezing your glutes, then lower.

Wall Sit Intermediate

Back against a wall, slide down until your thighs are parallel to the floor, and hold the position.

Bulgarian Split Squats Intermediate

Rear foot elevated on a chair, lower your front knee until it's bent around 90 degrees, then push up.

Step-Ups Intermediate

Step fully onto a sturdy chair or step with one foot, drive up, then step back down. Alternate legs.

Jump Squats Advanced

Perform a squat, then explode upward into a jump, landing softly back into the squat position.

Curtsy Lunges Advanced

Step one leg diagonally behind the other into a curtsy position, lower, then return to standing.

Chair Dips Beginner

Hands on the edge of a sturdy chair, legs extended, lower your body by bending your elbows, then push back up. Targets triceps.

Wall/Table Curls Beginner

Push against the underside of a sturdy table with your palms up as if curling, holding tension in the biceps.

Arm Circles Beginner

Arms extended out to the sides, make small controlled circles forward, then reverse direction.

Tricep Kickbacks (using a bottle) Intermediate

Hinge forward slightly, elbow bent at your side, extend your forearm straight back, then return.

Concentration Curls (using a bottle) Intermediate

Elbow braced against your inner thigh, curl a weighted bottle up toward your shoulder with control.

Pike Push-Ups Intermediate

Hips raised high forming an inverted V, lower your head toward the floor, then push back up. Targets shoulders and triceps.

Plank Shoulder Taps Advanced

From a plank, tap your opposite shoulder with each hand alternately while keeping your hips stable.

Diamond Push-Up Hold Advanced

Hold the bottom position of a diamond push-up for time to build tricep endurance and strength.

Plank Beginner

Forearms and toes on the ground, body in a straight line. Hold, keeping your hips level and core braced.

Dead Bug Beginner

Lying on your back, extend opposite arm and leg slowly while keeping your lower back pressed to the floor.

Side Plank Beginner

Balance on one forearm and the side of one foot, body in a straight line, hips lifted. Hold, then switch sides.

Bicycle Crunches Intermediate

Lying on your back, alternate bringing elbow to opposite knee in a pedaling motion.

Russian Twists Intermediate

Sit with knees bent, lean back slightly, and rotate your torso side to side, tapping the floor each time.

Leg Raises Intermediate

Lying on your back, legs straight, lift them toward the ceiling, then lower without touching the floor.

Hollow Body Hold Advanced

Lying on your back, lift shoulders and legs off the ground into a curved "hollow" shape, and hold.

V-Ups Advanced

Lying flat, simultaneously lift your legs and torso to touch your toes, forming a V shape, then lower.

Burpees Intermediate

Drop into a squat, kick back into a plank, do a push-up, jump feet back in, then jump up. Full-body conditioning.

Mountain Climbers Beginner

In a plank position, drive knees toward your chest alternately at a quick pace.

Jumping Jacks Beginner

Jump feet out while raising arms overhead, then jump back to start. Great warm-up or cardio finisher.

High Knees Beginner

Jog in place, driving your knees up toward your chest as quickly as you can with control.

Bear Crawl Advanced

On hands and feet, knees hovering just above the ground, crawl forward and backward with control.

Inchworms Intermediate

Bend and walk your hands forward to a plank, then walk your feet up to meet your hands, and repeat.

Skater Jumps Advanced

Leap sideways from one foot to the other in a skating motion, landing softly each time.

Squat to Press (using bottles) Advanced

Perform a squat, then as you stand, press two weighted bottles overhead.
